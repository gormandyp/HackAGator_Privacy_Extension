# PrivaC terms of use

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

These terms cover your use of the PrivaC Chrome extension ("PrivaC"), built by a student team at Gator Hack V (University of Florida, 2026). By installing or using PrivaC you agree to them. If you don't agree, remove PrivaC in `chrome://extensions`. How PrivaC handles your data is in the [privacy policy (PRIVACY.md)](PRIVACY.md).

## 1. What PrivaC is

PrivaC is free. There are no accounts, payments, ads or paid features. It shows which companies a website connects you to, gives the site a privacy grade from A to F, can decline optional cookies for you, and answers questions with an AI model that runs on your own computer.

## 2. Grades and answers are information, not advice

- A grade is PrivaC's own rules-based estimate from what your browser saw, public lists and the site's own policy. It is **not legal advice**, not a security guarantee, and not a finding that any site broke a law.
- A tracker loading means a company **could** collect data; it is not proof that data was collected or sold. Anything PrivaC couldn't check is marked "not checked yet", never "safe".
- A flag for a company or country on a U.S. government list means only that the company or server matches that public list on the date shown. It does not mean your data went to a government.
- C's AI answers can be wrong or out of date. Check anything important against the source PrivaC names next to it.

## 3. What PrivaC does for you on websites

When you allow it (or turn on **Protect me automatically**), PrivaC clicks a site's Reject button on cookie banners. It also clicks a button labeled "Do not sell or share my personal information" once per page when a site shows one. Declining cookies can change how a site works, for example remembered logins, preferences or embedded videos. You can turn C's pop-ups and automatic declining off in **Settings › C on websites**. Sites can change their banners at any time, so PrivaC cannot promise every choice is saved; it tells you when it couldn't confirm one.

**Clear this site's data** (in PrivaC's pop-up, after a second tap) has Chrome remove that one site's own cookies, saved data and cache. That signs you out of the site and resets its preferences. Other companies' tracking cookies stay.

## 4. Your part

- Use PrivaC only on your own browser, and follow the law and each website's terms.
- Don't use PrivaC, its data files or its AI to harass anyone, to get around a website's security, or to mislead people about a company.
- The AI model download (main version) is about 2 GB. Make sure your computer and internet plan can handle it before you click **Download C's brain**.
